About

I'm a technology director at HarperCollins Canada & Harlequin, part of the world's second-largest consumer book publisher. I own the strategy, architecture and delivery of our global Shopify Plus eCommerce platform, the enterprise applications behind it, and a growing set of applied AI products.

I started as a software engineer and still enjoy the details. That background helps me make sound architecture calls, earn the trust of engineering teams, and turn ideas into dependable systems that meet requirements and deadlines.

I build and keep high-performing teams by hiring well, developing people and giving them room to own their work. I'm known as a collaborative leader with strong integrity who works closely with marketing, finance, editorial and operations.

Interested in senior leadership roles in digital platforms, eCommerce and enterprise technology.
